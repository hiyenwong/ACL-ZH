# Information Equivalence Audit v0.2

Status: **PASS WITH LIMITATIONS**

The revised item set `items-v0.2.jsonl` was reviewed against the fact sheets after the v0.1 audit failures were corrected.

## Result

- 10 / 10 items preserve the intended fact set.
- No material extra-information finding remains.
- No material semantic-drift finding remains.
- The strongest category-label answer cues from v0.1 were removed.

## Remaining limitations

This is still an internal review, not an independent blinded semantic review.

Full ACL intentionally differs in segmentation and numbering. Those structural differences can affect lookup speed and are part of the experimental treatment. Results must not be described as a pure lexical effect.

The item set is also limited to short, basic technical explanations.

## Decision

`items-v0.2.jsonl` is a **human-study candidate**, not yet a validated instrument.

Before publication-quality human research, obtain at least one independent reviewer who is blind to the intended winning variant.
